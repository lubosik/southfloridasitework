Understanding Land Grading Costs Per Acre in Fort Lauderdale

Land grading is one of the most common site work activities in Broward County, essential for preparing properties for construction, improving drainage, or creating usable land. In Broward County, grading costs vary significantly based on the type of grading needed, soil conditions, flood zone requirements, and Broward County Natural Resources Protection Code permit requirements.

This comprehensive guide provides accurate 2025 pricing information for land grading per acre in Broward County, helping you budget effectively for your project. Whether you need rough grading for a large property in Weston or precision grading for building pads in Fort Lauderdale Beach, understanding these costs helps you plan your project budget.

Grading costs in Broward County are influenced by several factors unique to our region: high water tables, flood zone requirements, coastal soil conditions, strict environmental regulations, and county-specific permit requirements.

Average Land Grading Costs Per Acre in Fort Lauderdale

Land grading costs in Broward County typically range from $2,000 to $6,000 per acre for basic rough grading, and $4,000 to $12,000+ per acre for precision grading with drainage considerations. Coastal areas like Fort Lauderdale Beach and Hollywood Beach often have higher costs due to flood zone requirements and environmental regulations.

Here's a breakdown of average costs by grading type:

Rough Grading Costs Per Acre in Fort Lauderdale

Rough grading establishes general elevations and slopes across a property. This is the most basic form of grading, typically used for large properties or agricultural land preparation.

Rough grading costs in Broward County average $2,000 to $4,000 per acre. This includes:

  • Basic earthmoving to establish general slopes
  • Removal of high spots and filling of low areas
  • Basic compaction
  • No precision elevation requirements
For large properties (10+ acres), costs may be lower per acre due to economies of scale. Learn more about rough and final grading for new developments in Fort Lauderdale.

Final Grading Costs Per Acre in Fort Lauderdale

Final grading creates precise elevations needed for construction, drainage, or specific land uses. This requires more precision and typically costs $4,000 to $10,000 per acre in Broward County.

Final grading includes:

  • Precise elevation control to engineering specifications
  • Proper slope establishment for drainage
  • Compaction to required densities (typically 95% Proctor)
  • Fine grading and finish work
For building pad areas, final grading costs $2.00 to $5.00 per square foot. See our house pad and building pad grading services in Fort Lauderdale.

Grading with Drainage Considerations in Fort Lauderdale

Grading that includes drainage design and implementation costs more due to additional engineering and precision requirements. Expect $5,000 to $12,000+ per acre in Broward County.

This type of grading includes:

  • Drainage slope calculations and implementation
  • Integration with stormwater management systems
  • Swale and ditch creation
  • Proper outfall connections (must comply with Natural Resources Protection Code and SFWMD requirements)
Properties with existing drainage problems may require regrading for drainage problems in Fort Lauderdale, which can cost $6,000 to $18,000 per acre depending on the extent of regrading needed and Natural Resources permit requirements.

Factors Affecting Land Grading Costs in Broward County

Several factors significantly impact land grading costs in Broward County. Understanding these helps you get accurate estimates:

Soil Type and Conditions in Broward County

Broward County's diverse soil conditions affect grading costs:

  • Sandy soils (coastal areas): Easiest to grade, lowest cost ($2,000 to $4,000 per acre) - common in Fort Lauderdale Beach, Hollywood Beach, and coastal areas
  • Clay soils: Require more effort, moderate cost ($2,500 to $5,000 per acre)
  • Organic soils (muck): Must be removed and replaced, highest cost ($6,000 to $18,000+ per acre) - common in low-lying areas
  • Limestone bedrock: Requires specialized equipment, $4,000 to $10,000 per acre - may be encountered in inland areas

Soil testing before grading helps identify conditions and plan accordingly. In Broward County, high water tables also affect grading costs due to dewatering requirements. Natural Resources may require soil testing for certain projects.

Existing Topography in Fort Lauderdale

The existing slope and terrain significantly impact grading costs in Broward County:

  • Flat land: Easiest and cheapest to grade ($2,000 to $4,000 per acre) - common in many Broward County areas
  • Gentle slopes: Moderate cost ($2,500 to $5,000 per acre)
  • Steep slopes: Require more earthmoving and may need slope stabilization ($5,000 to $12,000+ per acre)
  • Very steep or rocky terrain: Most expensive ($10,000 to $25,000+ per acre)

Properties requiring significant cut and fill operations cost more than those needing minimal earth movement. Coastal areas like Fort Lauderdale Beach may require additional considerations for storm surge protection.

Water Table and Drainage Requirements in Fort Lauderdale

Broward County's high water tables and heavy rainfall require careful drainage planning. Properties with high water tables may need:

  • Additional fill material to elevate above water table
  • Dewatering during grading operations
  • Enhanced drainage systems
  • Specialized compaction techniques
These factors can add $1,500 to $6,000+ per acre to grading costs. Properties in flood zones (many Broward County properties are in Zone AE or VE) may require additional elevation, significantly increasing costs. According to FEMA flood zone maps, building pads must be elevated above Base Flood Elevation (BFE).

Site Access and Location in Broward County

Site access and location affect grading costs in Broward County:

  • Easy access (urban areas): Standard pricing applies - Downtown Fort Lauderdale, Las Olas, Hollywood
  • Limited access: May require smaller equipment, adding 10-20% to costs
  • Coastal areas: Fort Lauderdale Beach, Hollywood Beach, Pompano Beach may have additional environmental requirements adding 15-25% to costs
  • Natural Resources permit complexity: Properties requiring extensive Natural Resources Protection Code review may have additional compliance costs

Urban areas typically have better access and more competitive pricing, while coastal or environmentally sensitive areas may cost more due to permit complexity and environmental compliance.

Broward County Specific Factors

Several Broward County specific factors affect grading costs:

Natural Resources Protection Code Requirements

Broward County Natural Resources Protection Code permits are required for most grading projects and can add $1,000 to $10,000+ in permit fees and compliance costs. Natural Resources review can take 60-120 days, and projects must comply with environmental regulations.

Coastal properties in Fort Lauderdale Beach, Hollywood Beach, and Pompano Beach face additional mangrove protection and environmental compliance requirements that increase costs.

Flood Zone Elevation Requirements

Many Broward County properties are in FEMA flood zones (Zone AE or VE) requiring building pads to be elevated above Base Flood Elevation (BFE). This can require significant fill material, adding $2,000 to $10,000+ per acre to grading costs.

Coastal properties in Fort Lauderdale Beach, Hollywood Beach, Pompano Beach, and barrier islands face the strictest elevation requirements. According to FEMA flood zone maps, elevation requirements vary by location.

DER Standards

Broward County Department of Environmental Resources (DER) standards apply to grading work affecting environmental features. Compliance with DER standards may add complexity and cost to certain projects.

Additional Costs to Consider in Fort Lauderdale

Beyond basic grading, several additional costs may apply in Broward County:

  • Fill material: $20 to $60 per cubic yard if additional fill is needed (higher in coastal areas)
  • Compaction testing: $250 to $600 per test, typically 3-5 tests per acre (required by Broward County Building Division)
  • Engineering and surveying: $3,000 to $12,000+ for site plans and staking (Natural Resources-compliant plans required)
  • Natural Resources permits: $1,000 to $10,000+ depending on project scope and environmental impact
  • Erosion control: $750 to $3,000 per acre for silt fences and temporary measures (Natural Resources inspections required)
  • Drainage systems: $3,000 to $12,000+ per acre if drainage installation is included (must comply with Natural Resources Protection Code and SFWMD)
  • Tree removal: $1,500 to $6,000+ per acre if trees need to be removed before grading (Natural Resources tree removal permits required)
  • Dewatering: $2,000 to $10,000+ per acre for high water table conditions (very common in Broward County)

These additional costs can significantly increase total project costs. Always get detailed quotes that include all necessary work and Natural Resources Protection Code compliance.

Grading Costs by Project Type in Fort Lauderdale

Different project types require different grading approaches, affecting costs in Broward County:

Residential Property Grading in Fort Lauderdale

Residential grading in Broward County typically costs $2,500 to $6,000 per acre for basic grading, or $5,000 to $12,000+ per acre for precision grading with drainage.

For typical residential lots (0.25 to 1 acre) in Fort Lauderdale, total grading costs range from $1,500 to $12,000 depending on lot size and requirements. Building pad grading adds $4,000 to $25,000 depending on pad size and flood zone elevation requirements.
Custom home projects in areas like Fort Lauderdale Beach, Las Olas, or Hollywood Beach may require more extensive grading including access drive grading and comprehensive drainage systems.

Commercial Property Grading in Fort Lauderdale

Commercial grading projects in Broward County typically cost $4,000 to $10,000 per acre due to more extensive requirements:

  • Parking lot grading with proper slopes
  • Building pad preparation
  • Stormwater management integration (Natural Resources Protection Code and SFWMD compliant)
  • ADA compliance requirements
Parking lot and driveway grading typically costs $4,000 to $20,000 depending on size. Learn more about commercial site work in Fort Lauderdale.

Large-Scale Development Grading

Large-scale development grading (10+ acres) in Broward County typically costs less per acre due to economies of scale:

  • Basic rough grading: $1,500 to $3,000 per acre
  • Grading with drainage: $3,000 to $6,000 per acre
  • Precision grading: $4,000 to $9,000 per acre
Large projects may also require rough and final grading for new developments with road preparation and lot division. Natural Resources Protection Code permits for large developments can take 90-180 days.
