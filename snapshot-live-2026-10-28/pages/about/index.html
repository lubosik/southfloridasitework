About South Florida Site Work

Your trusted partner for coordinating comprehensive site work, earthwork, and land grading services across South Florida

Who We Are

South Florida Site Work is a service-area business that coordinates a vetted network of licensed contractors to deliver comprehensive site work, earthwork, and land grading services across Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach, and Monroe Counties.

We specialize in connecting general contractors, developers, custom home builders, commercial property owners, and high-end homeowners with the right licensed contractors who have the proper equipment, certifications, and expertise for their specific project needs. Our typical projects start around $25,000, and we handle everything from initial site development to final grading and drainage installation.

Unlike traditional contractors, we operate as a coordination and dispatch service, handling project intake, scoping, scheduling, and ongoing support. We then dispatch the nearest qualified, licensed contractor with the correct equipment and certifications to perform the field work, ensuring efficient service delivery across all of South Florida.

Our Service-Area Business Model

We operate as a service-area business, which means we coordinate work through a network of vetted, licensed contractors rather than maintaining our own fleet of equipment and crews. This model allows us to provide comprehensive coverage across all of South Florida while ensuring you get the right contractor for your specific project needs.

1

Project Intake & Scoping

We review your project requirements, site plans, and engineering drawings to understand the full scope of site work needed. Our team helps identify required permits, regulatory requirements, and potential challenges specific to your location and project type.

2

Contractor Matching

We match your project with licensed contractors from our vetted network who have the right equipment, certifications, and experience for your specific needs. We consider factors like project location, scope, timeline, and regulatory requirements to ensure the best fit.

3

Scheduling & Coordination

We coordinate scheduling, permit processing, and project sequencing to ensure your site work moves smoothly from start to finish. Our team handles communication between all parties and keeps you informed throughout the process.

4

Quality Assurance & Support

We monitor progress and ensure work meets engineering specifications, building code requirements, and permit conditions. Our team provides ongoing support and coordinates final inspections to ensure everything is completed to standards.

What We Coordinate

Our comprehensive site work services cover all phases of site development and preparation:

Site Work and Site Development

Complete site development packages from initial land clearing to final building pad preparation. We coordinate all phases including land clearing, mass excavation, grading, drainage, and utility preparation for commercial and residential projects.

Land Grading and Site Grading

Precision grading services for building pads, parking lots, roads, and drainage. We coordinate contractors with the expertise to achieve proper elevations and slopes, meeting engineering specifications and building code requirements.

Excavation and Earthwork

Mass excavation, cut and fill operations, utility trenching, and earthmoving services. We coordinate contractors with heavy equipment to handle large-scale earthwork projects, including foundation preparation and retention pond excavation.

Drainage and Stormwater Solutions

Comprehensive drainage and stormwater management systems for flood-prone and coastal properties. We coordinate contractors to design and install effective drainage solutions that meet South Florida Water Management District and local county requirements.

Land Clearing and Lot Preparation

Complete land clearing and lot preparation services including vegetation clearing, stump removal, grubbing, debris hauling, and rough grading. We coordinate contractors with the right equipment to prepare sites efficiently and in compliance with environmental regulations.

How to Get Started

Getting started with South Florida Site Work is simple:

  1. Contact Us: Reach out via email at support@firstclasscontractors.com or submit a quote request through our website. We'll discuss your project needs, timeline, and budget.
  2. Project Assessment: We review your project requirements, site plans, and any engineering drawings to understand the full scope of work needed. We help identify required permits and regulatory requirements.
  3. Quote & Proposal: We provide a detailed quote and proposal outlining the scope of work, timeline, and coordination services. Our quotes are transparent and include all coordination and contractor costs.
  4. Contractor Matching: Once you approve the proposal, we match your project with the right licensed contractor from our network and coordinate scheduling.
  5. Project Execution: We coordinate all phases of the project, handle communication, monitor progress, and ensure quality standards are met throughout.
  6. Final Inspection & Completion: We coordinate final inspections and ensure all work is completed to standards before your project is considered complete.
